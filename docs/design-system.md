# A shared visual language

Hosted Studio keeps the same workspace, controls, editor, settings navigation and materials as local Studio. Hosting uses the existing page heading, glass panels, fact grids and bounded file lists; show cloud ownership and queued work plainly. Keep save/conflict status in its established location. Generated public pages never include hosting controls, credentials or private workspace paths.

The Projects preview uses a bounded viewport inside its glass card. Its heading and help remain in normal flow, and longer public-page content scrolls inside the frame. Do not use a percentage frame height against a card that also contains surrounding text.

Studio's version badge sits beside the breadcrumb in the topbar. Use a quiet control inset with a short monospaced revision, condense to the revision on phones, and retain its full accessible label. Build details reuse the shared native dialog and typographic fact rows, with wrapping commit text and normal focus return.

Glass uses a web interpretation of Apple's Liquid Glass: clear hierarchy, luminous edges, translucent functional layers, and calm reading surfaces. Reference structure: <https://wenhaochai.com/>. Material guidance: <https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/materials>.

## Materials and scale

Regular glass belongs to functional panels, toolbars, floating navigation, inspectors, and the continuous article/About reading panel. Clear glass can overlay media with a supporting scrim. Keep foreground text sharp; code, expanded data and source editors can use denser insets. Avoid distorting text or putting multiple strong blur layers behind a reading paragraph.

Read [the Liquid Glass research and renderer contract](liquid-glass.md) before changing materials. The shared DOM runtime supplies real edge displacement in Chromium and a layered material fallback in WebKit. CSS blur alone is not the optical effect. Keep foreground text outside the refractive layer.

Write prefixed backdrop-filter declarations before their standard counterparts. Verify the computed blur in both Chromium and WebKit after bundling, since the generated CSS is what readers receive.

Materials inherit the selected atmosphere: cool blue for Sky, warm mineral for Sand, and green-gray for Mist, with corresponding dark tints. Navigation, cards, fields, and nested controls use this shared tint at different opacities. Keep the optical fill continuous to its edge; do not add an opaque white center or a separate inset panel. Article and About prose use `.article-body.reading-surface.glass-panel`: the shared glass gradient, blur, rim and shadow, with a translucent material tint instead of the dense gray reading fill. Functional glass may strengthen over wallpaper. The existing `readingOpacity` setting is shown as Article background tint and scales the unprotected glass tint beneath prose, through zero without a floor. Its saved key/range and dark-mode tint offset remain compatible; navigation/cards keep their own material.

Use three surface roles consistently:

| Role              | Use                                                  | Behavior                                                           |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Floating glass    | Navigation, outline, media controls                  | Refraction, thin luminous rim, bounded motion                      |
| Interactive glass | Plots, comparisons, form groups, linked cards        | Translucent environmental tint, blur, focus or hover edge feedback |
| Reading surface   | Prose, code, equations, expanded data, editor source | Translucent glass panel, denser code/data insets, no hover lift    |

Render each article as one continuous reading panel. Keep Markdown headings, paragraphs, lists, equations, and widgets in their original flow; do not split prose into cards or generated reading sections. Widgets use subtle glass insets; Mermaid figures and rich Markdown tabs stay transparent and unframed, while code and expanded data use denser tinted foundations for legibility. Keep charts and their controls stationary while adjusting them; hover changes their rim and depth. Only cards that open something lift on hover. The article panel has no hover lift.

Mermaid follows [the diagram contract](diagrams.md): clear figure/SVG/preview backgrounds, quiet structural node fills and rims, normal body-like ink, preserved authored/data colors and small shared glass controls. No full diagram panel, hover lift or optical distortion over text/geometry. Both themes and vector exports share the compiled presentation.
